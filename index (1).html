About Us

Elevating Every Player's Game

Ace Forward Tennis Academy was founded by two dedicated, varsity-level tennis athletes with a shared mission: make quality tennis coaching accessible, affordable, and genuinely impactful for players of every background and skill level.

Whether you've never held a racket or you're preparing for your school's varsity season, our coaches bring real competitive experience, structured lesson plans, and an unwavering commitment to your growth on the court.

Be Prepared

What to Bring & What We Provide

We want every session to run smoothly from the moment you arrive. Here's a quick breakdown of what's covered and what to pack.

We Provide

  • Tennis balls for the full session
  • Ball hopper & pickup equipment
  • Cones & training markers (as needed)
  • Structured drills & lesson plans
  • Court access coordination
  • Snacks or water if absolutely necessary

Students Should Bring

  • Your own tennis racket
  • Water bottle (stay hydrated!)
  • Athletic footwear (court shoes recommended)
  • Comfortable athletic clothing
  • Sunscreen for outdoor sessions
  • An open mind & great attitude 😊
💡 Don't own a racket yet? No problem — reach out to your coach before your session and we'll do our best to help arrange one for you. We never want equipment to be a barrier to getting on the court.
